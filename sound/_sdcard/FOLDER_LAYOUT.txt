Historical SD-card audio folder layout
The descriptions below translate the original folder notes.
They describe Russian/German recordings; the audio files are unchanged.
Use the current firmware mappings when checking actual track availability.

00: Not described.
01: Hours, Russian.
02: Minutes, Russian.
03: Day-of-month numbers, Russian.
04: Months, Russian.
05: Year.
06: Day of week.
07: Timer messages, Russian.
08: Music.
09: Numbers 000 through 030, Russian.
10: Reserved.
11: Hours, German.
    000: Noon.
    001: One o'clock, continuing through 023.
12: Numbers 000 through 059, German.
    000: Exactly/on the hour; 001 through 059: minute numbers.
13: Day-of-month ordinals 001 through 031, German, starting with "erste".
14: Months 001 through 012, German; original note also lists 000 as "Januar".
15: 003: Year 2023 in German; entries continue through 009.
16: 001: "Montag"; German weekdays.
17: German phrases (English meanings below).
    007: Timer set.
    008: Sound on.
    009: Sound off.
    014: Timer deactivated.
    015: "minuten" (minutes).
18: Clock chimes.
    000: Melody.
    001 through 012: Number of strikes.
19: Simple beeps.
    001: One beep.
    002: Two beeps.
20: Reserved.
21: Reserved.
