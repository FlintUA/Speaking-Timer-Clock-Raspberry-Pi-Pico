Settings

Volume and brightness apply as you move the sliders. LOCAL and Bluetooth have independent levels.

Choose the message independently from None, Signal or one music file in folder 08. Message only, sound only and silent completion are supported. Apply your choices before starting the timer. The German completion message is not yet available. Timer output uses its separate audio preference.

Bluetooth preferences fall back to LOCAL when the speaker is unavailable. Auto light uses night brightness from 22:00 to 07:00.

Close settings on the clock to apply browser changes. Stop playback before changing language or audio outputs.

Alarms

Stop playback or the timer and close editors on the clock before applying records. Times use Europe/Berlin.

Annual birthdays

Enter DD.MM, or DD.MM.YYYY if the birth year is known. Both repeat every year. February 29 is observed in leap years.
